Definitions & Methodology

How each metric is computed, where data comes from, and how to interpret it.

Data Flow

Entry: Data processors enter records processed daily — campaign, record count, platform, and their name.

Consolidation: Daily entries automatically roll up: Daily → Weekly (Sun-Sat) → Monthly → Quarterly → Yearly.

Costs: Platform invoices are entered monthly in the Platform Costs tab (Land Portal, Versium, Readymode).

Campaigns: Campaign names, tiers, and status come from the Command Center (cc.landai.ai/campaigns).

$/Record (Per Platform Stage)

Formula: Platform monthly spend ÷ Platform monthly records.

Calculated separately for Land Portal (data pull), Versium (skip trace), and Readymode (dialer/scrub) — never blended across platforms.

Why separate: Each platform processes a fundamentally different stage of the pipeline. A Land Portal "record" is not the same unit as a Versium or Readymode "record."

Blended $/Record

Formula: Total spend across all stages ÷ Clean Records that make it through the full pipeline.

Use: Shows the true all-in cost to produce one usable lead record after all processing stages.

Allocated Cost by Campaign

Formula: For each platform: (Campaign's platform records ÷ Total platform records) × Platform spend = Allocated cost for that platform. Sum across platforms for total campaign cost.

Use: Identifies which campaigns consume the most cost per platform. Compare against tier price to flag margin risk.

Clean Records

Formula: Land Portal records − Readymode (dialer) scrubbed-off records.

Meaning: The number of records that survived the full pipeline — pulled from Land Portal, skip-traced through Versium, and not scrubbed out by the dialer. These are the usable records delivered for calling.

Total Cost (Per Campaign)

Formula: Sum of allocated costs across all three platform stages (Land Portal + Versium + Readymode).

Use: The all-in data processing cost for a single campaign in a given month.

Platform Tool Reference

Land Portal: Data sourcing — pulls raw property records by county. Stage 1 of the pipeline.

Versium: Skip tracing — converts raw property records into contactable leads with phone numbers. Stage 2.

Readymode: Dialer + risk management/scrub — DNC scrubbing and call delivery. Stage 3. Records scrubbed off here reduce Clean Records.

Campaign Types

Direct: Campaigns where Land AI provides data + calling directly to the client.

Robin: Campaigns run through the Robin partnership channel.
